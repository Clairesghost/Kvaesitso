Radar clock: how the sweep can behave
The sweep line is the seconds hand. Same scope, five behaviours. Pictures are stills; the lit bar shows when something is drawing.
i. Continuous sweep
One full turn every 60 s. The line IS the seconds hand.
60 SECONDS ON THE CLOCK (lit = drawing)
about 720 frames a minute (12 per second)
ANIMATES
Only the sweep line and its fading trail inside the round scope.
STAYS STILL
Hour and minute hands (move once a minute), event blips, every other panel.
ii. Ping every 10 s
A quick turn (about 2 s), the trail fades, then the scope rests.
60 SECONDS ON THE CLOCK (lit = drawing)
about 144 frames a minute (80% fewer than i)
ANIMATES
The same sweep line, but only for about 2 s out of every 10 s.
STAYS STILL
Everything else. While resting, a small tick on the rim marks the current second.
iii. Ping every 5 s
The same quick turn, twice as often. A middle road.
60 SECONDS ON THE CLOCK (lit = drawing)
about 288 frames a minute (60% fewer than i)
ANIMATES
The sweep line for about 2 s out of every 5 s.
STAYS STILL
Everything else. Tick on the rim marks the current second between pings.
iv. Motion: Minimal
One tick per minute. No sweep at all.
60 SECONDS ON THE CLOCK (lit = drawing)
1 redraw a minute
ANIMATES
Nothing runs by itself. Once a minute the hands and blips step to the new time.
STAYS STILL
The sweep line (shown as a faint fixed line), all panels.
Motion: Off
Fully static picture.
60 SECONDS ON THE CLOCK (lit = drawing)
0 frames; redraws only when Home opens
ANIMATES
Nothing. Not even the radar.
STAYS STILL
Everything. It brings itself up to date when you come back to the Home screen.
Rules for every option
Motion runs only when ALL are true:
• the Home screen is showing
• the screen is on
• Battery Saver is off
• battery above 15% or charging
• the phone’s remove-animations setting is off

It never goes above 12 frames a second, only the round scope moves, and a Motion setting (Full / Minimal / Off) lets you turn it down. Full = the option you pick above; Minimal = iv; Off = static.

Frame counts are design arithmetic, NOT measurements.

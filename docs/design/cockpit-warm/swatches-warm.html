Fonts (all SIL Open Font License 1.1, licence text copied into fonts/)

Pair 1 (kept from the cockpit): Share Tech Mono (numbers, labels) + Rajdhani (text). Technical, narrow, a bit cold.
Pair 2 (proposed, softer and warmer): DM Mono (numbers, labels) + Nunito (text). Rounded letter ends, friendlier, still clearly a readout. See fonts-warm.png.

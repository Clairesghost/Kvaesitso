Cute vintage phone

Exactly "1 · Cute vintage phone" from the Two Dials Compared page, on its own, as the base to work from. Drag or flick the ring to spin it, tap an app, type in the search bar, tick a task. Where it stands: the dial is as it was in the original; the weather also shows on the date tab as an icon and temperature, as an icon and temperature on the ivory paper tab. The three coin rings on the search bar are the status gauges: Wi-Fi (or mobile signal when off Wi-Fi), Bluetooth and battery. Under the calendar, a small ALERTS block shows the latest notifications with quick actions (Reply, Archive, View or Open, and a cross to dismiss); tap ALERTS for the full Today sheet. The ring round the camera hole is the status light: amber = notifications, green = charging, red = battery low. In the sheet you can dismiss, clear all, mark messages read, and choose per app whether a preview shows.

Where the features hide

  • 12 apps: the ring turns; icons stay upright.
  • Clock: small, in the centre, with air around it.
  • Weather: the band round the clock tints with the sky.
  • Calendar: the card under the dial, with next event and countdown.
  • Tasks / notes: tabbed card below it.
  • Search: try 12*7, t buy milk, n idea, map.

Camera hole

  • The hole is the rivet in a chrome boss, like a phone's bell cap. No status light (removed, D-126).
  • Real hole position still has to be measured on your S20 FE.

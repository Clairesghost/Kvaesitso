Gaslight Dial

Clockpunk / gothic steampunk and gaslamp fantasy, in quieter colours: aged brass, leather, bottle green and oxblood, with amber gas lamps beside the status pill, warm fog at the bottom, pointed gothic arches round the calendar date and the favourites, and the working gears. Same features as before: spin the ring, tap an app, press and hold for shortcuts, pull up for all apps, pull down for quick settings. It is a picture that moves, not the real launcher.

          What this does not prove

          • It is a web page, not your phone. The ring, quick settings and menus are new code and need a phone test first.
          • Torch and sound mode really can be switched by a launcher. Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Battery saver and brightness only open Android's own page (facts AF-8, AF-21). Rotation needs one permission.
          • App buttons take their colours from the season and light/dark look you pick. Apps that are not on the ring would get an automatic matching look.
          • The status pill sits where this phone's front camera hole is. Where exactly the hole is differs by phone, so the first real build must measure it on yours (fact AF-3) before the pill is lined up. The icons mirror quick-settings state here; reading real Wi-Fi and Bluetooth state on the phone is unchecked.
          • "Follow phone" here follows your computer's light or dark setting, as a stand-in for the phone's.
          • Notifications, music, weather, calendar and tasks are made-up demo data. The real ones need your permission and your yes on weather source and cost first.
          • Whether a drag on the ring clashes with the launcher's own swipes is still unchecked. Battery use is not measured.

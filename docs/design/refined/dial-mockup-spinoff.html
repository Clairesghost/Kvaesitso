Chrome dial spin-off

Drag the ring to spin it, flick it to let it coast, tap a disc to open an app. Same dial as the original mock-up, with domed chrome-and-brass 3D icons and a calm engine-turned clock face instead of the painted city. Tap the lower panel, or swipe it sideways, to flip between agenda, tasks and alerts. Press "Music demo" to see the track name scroll slowly round the dial. This is a picture that moves, not the real launcher.

    What this does not prove

    • It runs in a web page, not on your phone. The real ring would be new code and needs a phone test first.
    • Whether a drag on the ring clashes with the launcher's own swipes is still unchecked.
    • The click is a sound here. On the phone it would be a small vibration, and Samsung may not play it.
    • Battery use is not measured. Here the page only draws while the ring moves; the real one would do the same.

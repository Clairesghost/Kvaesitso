Try these

  • Spin: drag or flick the ring; tap an app.
  • Short pull: drag the chrome top bar down a little, or tap it. Notifications (counts only) and four quick buttons.
  • Long pull: keep dragging, or tap the lip. All buttons, brightness, now-playing card.
  • Side panel: tap the small chrome tab on the right edge, or drag it left. Shortcuts, recents, notes and tasks.
  • App list: tap "All apps", use the A–Z strip.
  • Search: 12*7, t buy milk, n idea, map.

Camera hole

  • The rivet of the chrome boss at the top. It breathes amber while notifications wait and glows green on charge, only while the phone is awake and Motion is Full.
  • The real hole position still has to be measured on your S20 FE.

Not real yet

  • Weather, calendar, notifications and music are made up.
  • Brightness here only dims this page. The real one needs a "Modify system settings" permission.
  • Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and Battery Saver buttons: Android only lets a launcher open their pages, so on the phone they may not flip in place.
  • App-list icons are placeholders until the icon style is chosen.
  • Real finger touch, swipes against Android's own gestures, and tap sizes on the phone are untested.

Lock screen picture: four ideas

A launcher cannot change Samsung's lock screen, but we can supply the wallpaper picture behind it. Each idea is shown for Day (cherry) and Night (black lacquer). The dashed outlines are where Samsung draws things (clock and alerts at the top, camera hole, shortcut icons in the bottom corners, swipe area at the bottom); the picture keeps those areas quiet. No animation, and the night pictures are mostly black to save battery.

Fonts (page and picture): Oleo Script Swash Caps, Libre Franklin, DM Sans, all from Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font Licence. The final wallpaper file will have the words drawn as shapes, so nothing needs installing. Mock-ups only; the real picture is made after you pick.

# Task brief: B2-home

One task = one stage = one branch `claude/B2-home`. Read only this, `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/design-system.md` and your row in `ops/STATUS.md`. For exact shapes and sizes, Grep the mock-up `docs/design/refined/dial-mockup-cute.html` for the part you build (housing, dial, clock, enamel plate); never read it whole. Search the code with Grep and open only matching files.

Goal: replace Kvaesitso's home screen with the Vintage Pocket Dial home: the chrome top housing, the dial with 12 app discs that spins, and the clock with the enamel time plate, in Day and Night. Implements D-102 to D-105, D-122, D-124 to D-126, D-128, D-132 to D-134, D-142, D-151, D-163, D-165, D-173, D-180 to D-182, and the layout and motion rules of `docs/design-system.md`.

Files in scope (new files in `de.mm20.launcher2.ui.vpd` and subpackages; upstream edits tiny, each in its own commit):
- Home hook: a new `ScaffoldComponent` (e.g. `VpdHomeComponent`) chosen in `SharedLauncherActivity.kt` (the `homeComponent =` line, AF-22). Provide `LocalVpdColors` there from `VpdLookSettings.look` + system dark mode (B1 tokens, `VpdLookResolver.kt`). Hide the status bar via the scaffold configuration (AF-18); our housing replaces it.
- Layout: stage 360 x 780 units scaled to the screen width, positions from the design system "Layout" line. Read system bar and cutout insets yourself (AF-13). Space under the dial: plain phone body (D-181), no cards. Chevron at 706-750 opens Kvaesitso's existing search page with the full app list (AF-5).
- Top housing: one chrome plate. Left: search slot with hint "Dial, find, sum, jot…" (D-105); tap opens Kvaesitso's existing search with the keyboard (AF-5). Centre: camera ring around the real camera hole (position from the cutout, AF-3, measure, do not hard-code); tap opens the camera; its light shows green when charging and red when low (D-125). Right: three coins: connection (Wi-Fi / mobile / none, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE already declared), Bluetooth (dark when something is connected, grey when not, D-122; needs BLUETOOTH_CONNECT, asked once with a short plain explanation, D-182; if refused, grey with no nagging), battery (level, plain dark ring, AF-19). Coin taps open the matching settings page (AF-8). Use broadcasts / callbacks registered only while Home is visible, no polling (AF-15).
- Dial: dark plate, thin ivory ring round the red centre band, 2 px outer chrome rim, static tick marks that do NOT turn (D-142), chrome finger stop bottom right with the cream lamp glow at night only (D-151). 12 flat ivory discs, cherry rims, app icons kept upright (AF-23). Apps: favourites, pinned first then most used (D-180), via the existing favourites service; fewer than 12 = empty discs. Tap a disc: press effect, launch about 120 ms later (instantly when animations are off, AF-9) through `LauncherApp.launch` + `FavoritesService.reportLaunch` (AF-23). Drag spins the ring, flick with friction (decay animation that ends by itself), snap to a slot; the ring returns home 5 s after it stops (one timer, only while visible, cancelled by touch, instant when Motion is Off, D-126). The dial must consume its taps and drags so the scaffold's swipe and double-tap do not fire on it (AF-22); swipes on the empty body keep Kvaesitso's gestures.
- Clock: in the centre; Day = cherry ring, ivory face (D-173); Night = wine ring, black face, ivory ticks and hands, amber pin (D-163). No date window (D-124). Enamel plate at six o'clock with 12-hour time in the script font, e.g. "10:42 pm", hands never reach it (D-165). Updates once per minute from a lifecycle-aware ticker, only while visible (AF-15). Hold half a second opens the Clock app (D-139). Tap does nothing in this stage.
- Settings stored, no screen yet (in `core/preferences`, like `VpdLookSettings`): Motion Full / Minimal / Off (default Full) and Ring returns home On / Off (default On). Motion Off = no spin animation (snap at once), no press animation.

Done when (visible on the phone):
- Home shows the cherry phone body, chrome housing at the top with search, camera ring around the real camera hole and three coins; the dial with your favourite apps; the clock with the time plate.
- Spinning the dial works with a finger, it snaps, it returns home after 5 s; tapping a disc opens that app.
- Battery and connection coins are real; Bluetooth goes dark when headphones connect.
- Phone in dark mode shows the Night look.
- Search slot and chevron open the existing search and app list; swiping on the empty body still does what it did.

Out of scope: cards (B3), our own app list, search results, settings screens, dial editor, quick timer pop-up, first-run, About (B4), weather and landscape (B5), click feel and sounds (B6). Do not change Kvaesitso's other screens.
Android calls needed: AF-3, AF-5, AF-8, AF-9, AF-13, AF-15, AF-18, AF-19, AF-20, AF-22, AF-23 (all UNVERIFIED on this phone). Ask `api-scout` for: reading Bluetooth "connected" state on Android 13 with BLUETOOTH_CONNECT; opening the camera; opening the Clock app. Write AF lines for what you learn.

Goal: our own app list, our own Settings page and the dial editor, in the cream / black sheet style, plus the four colour variations. Implements D-135, D-138, D-141, D-143, D-168, D-171, D-177, D-178, D-180 (until edited), D-189 (calendar choice), D-195, and the sheet rules in `docs/design-system.md` ("Rules for any new screen" 2).

Files in scope (new files in `de.mm20.launcher2.ui.vpd.*`; upstream edits tiny, each in its own commit):
1. Sheet shell: a full-screen sheet over Home below the housing (left/right 6, top 54, bottom 6), cream gradient by day, near-black with chrome rim at night, script title top left, ✕ or Done top right, scrolls inside. Back and the Home button close it (AF-7). Opening/closing slides only when the motion gate allows; otherwise it just appears.
2. App list (D-171, D-168): opened by the chevron AND by swipe up on the empty body (replace the B2 chevron action; set Home's swipe-up to open it, keep other swipes as they are). Title "Apps", "Find an app" field at the top (filters by name as you type; this is NOT the B4b search), "Launcher settings" row, category chips across the top (All first), then sections per category, icon grid 4 per row (ivory discs like the dial, app name under), right-hand letter strip with a big letter bubble while sliding; sliding jumps to the first app with that letter and glows every app with that letter for about 1.6 s (glow only when the gate allows; otherwise a static highlight that clears on release). Apps from Kvaesitso's `AppRepository` (all launchable apps, work profile included the way Kvaesitso does). Categories automatic from Android's app category (`ApplicationInfo.category`), names: Games, Audio, Video, Photos, Social, News, Maps, Productivity, Accessibility, Other (no category = Other). Hold an app (half a second) for a small menu: "Move to…" (pick a category; saved), "Put on the dial" (goes to the editor), "App info" (Android's app details page). Tap an app: press effect and launch 120 ms later (AF-9), launch counter (AF-23). Saved category moves live in `core/preferences` (a map package -> category), like `VpdHomeSettings`.
3. Settings page (D-138), opened from the app list row: groups in this order, rows in Libre Franklin, group titles in script:
   - Look: Day or night look (Auto / Day / Night, stored in B1's `VpdLookSettings`), Colour (Cherry / Mint / Midnight / Powder, D-177: new token sets in `de.mm20.launcher2.ui.vpd` changing the phone body, the clock ring, the dial plate and accents; chrome, ivory discs and cards stay; values from the mock-up CSS; new stored field `vpdColour`).
   - Motion: Full / Minimal / Off (B2's `VpdHomeSettings.motion`).
   - Dial: "Choose the 12 apps" (opens the editor), Ring returns home On/Off (B2 setting). Click feel and Click sound are NOT shown yet (they come with B6 when they work).
   - Alerts: notification access status row (opens Android's page), and "Show on the Alerts card" per app (switch list of apps that have posted notifications; hidden apps are filtered from the Alerts card; stored set of packages).
   - Calendars (D-189): a switch per calendar on the phone (from Kvaesitso's `CalendarRepository` calendar list); hidden ones are left out of the Calendar card; stored set.
   - Go back to your old launcher: three plain steps (Settings > Apps > Choose default apps > Home app > One UI Home; mark the menu names as "check on your phone" in the handoff) and a button that opens Android's Home app chooser page (`Settings.ACTION_HOME_SETTINGS`).
   - More launcher settings (D-195): one plain row at the bottom that opens Kvaesitso's own `SettingsActivity` (AF-12).
4. Dial editor (D-141, D-143): a mini dial in the sheet (same drawing code as the Home dial, smaller), drag a disc onto another to swap, tap a disc to replace it from a list of the other apps (with Find); an empty disc can be filled. Opened from Settings > Dial and by holding a disc on the Home dial for half a second (this hold must not start a spin; a drag still spins). Changes show on Home at once. Saved as an ordered list of 12 app keys in `core/preferences`; once saved, Home uses this list instead of favourites (D-180 applies only while nothing is saved). An uninstalled app leaves an empty disc.
5. Colour tokens: extend `VpdColors` with the colour variation (Cherry default), so Home, cards, sheets all follow it. Keep Day/Night logic.

Done when (visible on the phone):
- Chevron and swipe up open the app list; chips, sections and the letter strip work; holding an app moves it to another category and it stays there after a restart.
- Settings opens from the app list; changing Look, Colour, Motion and Ring returns home changes Home at once and survives a restart.
- Hiding an app's alerts or a calendar removes it from the card.
- The dial editor swaps and replaces discs; holding a Home disc opens it; the choice survives a restart.
- "More launcher settings" opens Kvaesitso's settings; "Go back" opens Android's Home app page.
